# ₿ Crypto Market Trends

An interactive web-based cryptocurrency analytics dashboard for exploring historical price movements, trading volume, market capitalization, and market trends across multiple cryptocurrencies.

The project transforms historical cryptocurrency market data into an interactive dashboard with cryptocurrency and year-based filtering, KPI cards, and visual analytics.

## 🚀 Live Dashboard

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://crypto-market-trends.vercel.app

🔗 **GitHub Repository:** https://github.com/nikhil300705/crypto-market-trends

---

## 📊 Project Overview

Crypto Market Trends provides an interactive view of historical cryptocurrency market activity across multiple digital assets.

The dashboard allows users to explore:

- Cryptocurrency price movements
- Opening and closing prices
- High and low prices
- Trading volume
- Market capitalization
- Historical price trends
- Cryptocurrency comparisons
- Year-wise market behavior

The goal is to make historical cryptocurrency data easier to explore and understand through an interactive web-based analytics experience.

- Close price

- Low price

- Price movements
- Market volatility
- High and low periods
- Changes between opening and closing prices

---

### 📊 Market Metrics

The dashboard analyzes several important cryptocurrency indicators:

#### Trading Volume

Tracks the amount of cryptocurrency traded during the selected period.

#### Market Capitalization

Provides a view of the historical market value associated with each cryptocurrency.

#### Price Range

Compares high and low prices to understand historical price movement.

#### Open vs Close

Compares opening and closing prices to identify daily market movement.
